## 1.0 INTRODUCTION

Generic Operation Performance Standard (GOPS) is a guideline for Upstream Assets to establish the associated Performance Standards (PS) of Safety Critical Elements (SCE) whereby describing the essential minimum requirements that must be performed on the equipment or system to maintain the integrity throughout the asset's operational life. The important requirements are the SCE goals, functional performance, minimum acceptance criteria, assurance task and its frequency.

The performance standards in this revision are now more focused on critical assurance tasks and updated according to new requirements from statutory, international standards, PCSB's internal guidelines, latest technologies, and best practices. They are now more value driven whereby allocation for risk-based, condition-based, and data driven strategies are expanded, aligning to U-IMAGe, OEMS 2.0, and the latest PCSB Mechanical Integrity Procedure.

## 1.1 Objectives

The objective of this document is to define the minimum assurance tasks for Safety Critical Elements (SCE) and in turn, facilitate the formulation of the respective location's Site-Specific Performance Standards (SSPS).

This GOPS document has been approved by the respective discipline Technical Authority as each SCE Group in this GOPS is owned by the Technical Authority. The name of the Technical Authority which is stated in each GOPS sheet is intentionally included for ease of cross-references and communication.

Any proposed change or improvement is to be highlighted to the document custodian (IVA) and the respective SCE Group's Technical Authority.

## 1.2 Note to Users

This Generic Operation Performance Standard (GOPS) is developed based on the following guiding principles focusing on SCE functionality:

- Measurable – Assurance tasks have measurable performance acceptance criteria
- Align – Assurance tasks align with SCE functionality and SCE goal
- Executable – Assurance tasks are executable

Maintenance tasks which are not related to functionality assurance of the SCE are excluded from the Performance Standard and retained as normal maintenance activities that are determined through the Equipment Reliability Strategy (ERS) process as per Asset Integrity Management System (AIMS).

## 1.3 Variation to GOPS Requirements

The following may present a reason to deviate against the GOPS requirements or present an alternate PS assurance tasks in the SSPS:

- Risks posed may not be as apparent / prevalent (i.e. medium and low as per Petronas HSE Risk Matrix) in an unmanned installation
- Deviations supported by sound engineering judgement, calculations, risk assessment / analysis, etc.
- Inapplicable GOPS assurance task rising from unsusceptible failure mechanism / mode of the task for the site (e.g. H2S corrosion related failures that are not susceptible to the normal operating parameters of the site's equipment or system).
- A facility is selected to undergo a Run-to-End or Operation Simplification strategies.
- The assurance task is similarly executed based on other requirements, e.g. Class Society Rules for floating production / storage facilities.
- A more stringent regulatory requirement compared to the GOPS performance standard

## 1.4 Site-Specific Performance Standards (SSPS)

Notwithstanding to the variation stated in Section 1.3, minor changes to the GOPS Assurance Tasks and its frequency in the SSPS is acceptable due to the availability of a more relevant, applicable and practical practice (which shall be supported by relevant references). Similarly, such change shall require endorsement and approval by relevant parties as per the respective Mechanical Integrity procedure.

For tasks with frequency of less than one month (e.g. weekly), work order planning can be done monthly while task execution and reporting in PMMS can be based on weekly.

The Responsible Party of the assurance task in the GOPS is to facilitate asset's assignment of the work centre in PMMS, based on normal practice. It does not however dictate the way asset have presently assigned the tasks (i.e. work centre).

Details for the overall SCE Management Process can be referred in the PCSB Mechanical Integrity (MI) Procedure.

## LS004 Alternate Means of Escape
### PETRONAS OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE STANDARD AND ASSURANCE TASK

**SCE GROUP:** LS004 Alternate Means of Escape (Liferafts, Scramble Nets and Ladders to Sea)

**SCE GOAL:**
- To provide, as far as is reasonably practicable, for the safe escape of all persons from the installation in case arrangements for evacuation fail.

**BARRIER AUTHORITY DISCIPLINE:** Maritime Safety

### FUNCTIONALITY

| Functional Criteria | Assurance Task | Responsible Party | Minimum Acceptable Criteria | Frequency | Measurable Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. To provide means to facilitate all personnel for safe escape on the water when primary and secondary means are unavailable. | 1.1 Perform Liferafts and Means for Descent to Sea Equipment / Devices Monthly Assessment.<br><br>i. Assess the liferaft condition:<br>• Correct numbers of liferafts and capacity in designated locations as per safety equipment layout.<br>• The painter shall be secured to a sufficiently strong part of the installation.<br>• Liferafts certificates shall be valid<br><br>ii. Assess that means for descent to sea (scramble net/knotted rope/rope ladder/ladders to sea, etc.) are in a safe operable condition with correct numbers and location as described in the safety equipment layout, and assess for signs of deterioration at the anchor points / shackles. | Safety Officer | i. Liferaft certificate is valid and in safe operable condition<br><br>ii. Means for descent to sea in a safe operable condition | 1M | Y/N |

### AVAILABILITY

All tertiary means of escape shall be available at all times.

### RELIABILITY

All tertiary means of escape shall be reliable at all times.

### SURVIVABILITY

Tertiary means of escape shall be available post MAE.
